Who I am

I'm an Interior Designer and Creative from Seville, Spain. After completing my studies in Interior Design, including an academic year in Rome, I've developed a strong interest in commercial spaces, hospitality projects and experiential events. What draws me to these disciplines is the same thing every time: the opportunity to make a space tell a story.

A stint in Copenhagen at a luxury interior and product design studio introduced me to a completely different way of working: more ergonomic, rational and human-centred. That contrast between Mediterranean sensibility and Nordic design culture helped me understand something I'd already been feeling: that my work is shaped by that mix. I collect references, materials, atmospheres and ways of living from every place I pass through, and I believe that accumulation is what gives a space its character. In the end, what makes a space feel like something is everything the designer has gathered along the way: places, images, different understandings of how people live.

Alongside interior design, I've always been drawn to the audiovisual world. My experience as art director on a feature film deepened my understanding of how visual atmospheres and narratives are built, a sensibility that now directly shapes the way I conceive and design spaces.

How I work

To design is to solve and to provoke. A space must function flawlessly, responding to real and concrete needs; yet excellent design is never indifferent: it invites people to feel, to think, and to engage with their surroundings in a completely new way.

My process begins by defining the emotional impact of a space before drawing the first line. I explore the precise tension between structure and temporality: how a carefully composed layout can transform through atmosphere, movement, and human experience. When function and emotion align, design alters how we inhabit and perceive the world.

Operating at the intersection of interior architecture, event design, and spatial storytelling, I view circulation not merely as a functional requirement, but as a narrative tool. It is within this crossover that spaces cease to be simple containers and become immersive, unfolding experiences.

La 3000 Maravillas
— Feature Film

Location

Sevilla, Spain

Type

Art Direction · Feature Film

Role

Art Director

Year

2025

Core Tasks

Diseño de Sets
Selección de Localizaciones
Manejo de Presupuesto
Dirección de Equipo

Bajo un contexto de bajo presupuesto, la dirección de arte se planteó como un ejercicio de máxima optimización y agudeza creativa. El diseño visual se apoyó en la intervención de localizaciones reales para contraponer la crudeza de una realidad precarizada con destellos surrealistas y humorísticos. Coordinando estrechamente al equipo y los recursos disponibles, logramos dotar a los sets de una identidad costumbrista, expresiva y de alto impacto que enraíza y potencia el relato cinematográfico.
